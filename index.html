Cost estimation process

A consistent, four-stage sequence — the same order every estimate moves through, from drawing to final quote.

01

Quantity Takeoff

Measuring quantities directly from architectural, plumbing and electrical drawings, item by item.

02

Market Price Check

Sourcing current supply costs from Australian hardware suppliers and up-to-date local labour rates.

03

Quantity & Price Verification

Cross-checking every quantity and rate against the drawings to catch errors before they reach a quote.

04

Review & Finalization

A final pass over the full estimate before it's finalized and issued as a quote.
